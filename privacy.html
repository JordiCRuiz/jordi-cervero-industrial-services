How contact enquiries are handled.

Jordi Cervero Ruiz, reachable at jordicerveroruiz@gmail.com, uses the information submitted through the contact form solely to respond to your enquiry and manage any resulting professional relationship.

The form collects your name, email, optional company and telephone, area of interest and message. The legal basis is your request for contact and, where applicable, steps before a professional engagement. Required fields are marked on the form.

Service provider and retention

Netlify Forms processes contact submissions and may send email notifications to the configured recipient. Review the applicable service terms and data processing arrangements during deployment. Enquiries are retained only for as long as needed to respond and meet applicable obligations.

Your rights

To request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, write to the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
